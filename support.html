Help

I bought the full career on another device
Tap the gear, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
Playing on a new iPhone or iPad
With iCloud on and the same Apple ID, the title screen offers the career from your other device.
The text is too small
Gear > Text > Large.
The device runs hot
Gear > Graphics: Low or Medium.
Ads
Ads only play when you choose one for a reward, and the full career turns them off. After an ad, Gear > Report an ad emails me about it. Gear > Privacy choices changes your ad consent where the law asks for one.
Starting over
Gear > Reset everything. It resets this device only, and keeps any cash you bought.
