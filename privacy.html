Urahara 2000

Privacy policy. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Urahara 2000 is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Scott Yeung. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. I don't collect, see or sell your personal data. The one outside company that gets data from the game is Google, which shows the optional reward ads, and only to players who haven't bought the full career.

What stays with you

  • Your career, settings, best exits and purchases are saved on your device.
  • If you use iCloud, your career and best exits go between your own devices through Apple's iCloud. I can't see them.
  • Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple under your Game Center account.
  • Purchases go through Apple. I never see your name or payment details; Apple only tells the game which items you own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Free players can choose to watch a video ad for an in-game reward. An ad only plays when you tap to watch one. Once you've bought the full career, the game stops starting the ad software.
  • To show ads and measure them, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), other identifiers limited to this app, your rough location from your IP address, the ads you saw and how you interacted with them, and crash and performance data.
  • The game asks once, with Apple's prompt, whether Google may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, and wherever else the law asks for it, Google's consent form asks you first. You can change your answer in the game's Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content rated for teens.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Email

If you email me for help, or with "Report an ad" in the game's Settings (it adds the ad's id), I use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when we're done.

Children

The game is rated 13+ and isn't made for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the game's data on a device with Settings > Reset everything in the game, or by deleting the app. iCloud copies are under iOS Settings > your name > iCloud.
  • To reach the data Google holds, use Google's own tools: My Ad Center.
  • If you're in the UK or the EU you have rights over your personal data, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
